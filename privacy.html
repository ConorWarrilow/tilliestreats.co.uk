Who we are

Tillie’s Treats is a small home bakery based in Guildford, Surrey. You can contact us at hello@tilliestreats.co.uk.

What we collect

When you enquire about an order we collect the details you send us: your name, email address, and the information you provide about your order such as date, guest numbers, design references and dietary needs.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and fulfil an order you place. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your consent.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and order correspondence is kept for as long as needed to manage the order and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, then deleted.

Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except where necessary to deliver an order (for example a delivery courier) or where required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us at the email address above and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies. Your theme preference (light or dark) is stored locally in your browser and is never sent to us.
