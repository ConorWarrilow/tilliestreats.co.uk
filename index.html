FAQ

Good things to know

How far in advance should I order?

As much notice as you can — popular dates book up weeks ahead, especially spring and summer weekends. For larger cakes and weddings, three to six weeks is ideal. It’s always worth asking about shorter notice in case there’s a slot.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A deposit secures your date and is deducted from the final balance. The balance is due before collection or delivery. Deposits are non-refundable, but can usually be moved to another date with enough notice.

Can you cater for allergies and dietary needs?

Eggless and dairy-free sponges can be arranged with notice. Please note the kitchen is not a nut-free or gluten-free environment, so cakes cannot be guaranteed free from traces — always mention allergies when you enquire.

Do you deliver?

Collection is from Guildford. Local delivery within Surrey can be arranged for a fee based on distance; tiered cakes are always delivered rather than collected. A confirmed address is shared once your order is booked.

How should I store and serve the cake?

Keep it cool and out of direct sun. If refrigerated, bring it back to room temperature for 30–60 minutes before serving so the sponge and buttercream soften. Most cakes are best within three to four days.

Can I change my order after booking?

Adding extras is usually fine if time allows. Reducing the size or cancelling within two weeks of the date may be subject to a charge, as ingredients and planning are already underway.
